1 to 7: the same page in seven looks

  • 1Calm violetLight and airy, closest to the app. White page, the real chat screen on a soft violet panel, violet only for the button and one phrase, mint only to mean done.
  • 2Fresh mintA mint ground with dark text and a dark button; violet only on the word "Penny". The app screen hangs out of the mint band onto white.
  • 3Big typeA white page where a very large black headline is the picture, with "charge" in violet. The app screen rises beside its last line.
  • 4Night violetDark by design: a flat deep violet ground, light text, mint for small highlights, the app shown in its dark mode. No glow, no gradient.
  • 5Job siteWarm paper tones and heavy headlines for the trades, with a large slot for a real crew photo (the dashed frame is a note for you, not part of the page).
  • 6Receipt paperThe hero is a paper till receipt with a torn edge, the headline printed in a typewriter face, the steps printed like receipt lines.
  • 7Penny up frontFriendly: soft violet and mint shapes, rounded corners, and a larger drawn Penny waving toward the app screen.

8 to 14: seven different page structures

  • 8Chat with PennyThe page is one conversation: Penny's messages carry every fact, your questions sit in grey bubbles on the right, and the button is a reply chip. On a desktop it looks like a messaging app.
  • 9Editorial long readA magazine story: a nameplate, a big serif headline and one example crew's month in short paragraphs, with the numbers in the margin and Penny's line as a pull quote.
  • 10Bento gridTiles of different sizes, each a small live demo: a card alert turning into a matched receipt, an email arriving, a job picked, the morning brief. The violet tile is the button.
  • 11Cinematic scrollOne receipt is the hero: each screen is one shot of its trip, from a crumpled photo in the camera to a filed line in Payments, with one big caption under each picture.
  • 12Two sidesA seam down the middle: the owner's month now (texts to the crew, faded receipts, a sheet with gaps) against the same moment with Penny. On a phone a switch flips between them.
  • 13A workdayOne Monday on a clock: 7:02 AM the card alert, 10:40 AM a photo from the truck, 4:12 PM the chat, a dark overnight band, then the 8:00 AM brief. The page goes from morning light to night.
  • 14Swiss utilityPlain tables you read in one look: four charges and whether each has its receipt, a spec sheet, a "never" table and the planned prices. Monospaced numbers on a strict grid.

15 to 21: seven different messages

  • 15The ShoeboxFor the solo owner: "Too small for big accounting software?" Penny empties your receipt shoebox for tax time.
  • 16The answered letterFor the bookkeeper: "Delete the 'what was this charge?' email." Penny answers it before you send it.
  • 17Point and ShootFor the owner on the go: "Done on the walk to your car." Penny asks, you snap, she files.
  • 18Your morning messageFor the owner who never opens the app: "One message a morning. That's your part."
  • 19Job BoardFor the contractor costing a job: "Every receipt on its job." Receipts drop into job lanes.
  • 20Printed promiseFor the careful owner: "Penny never asks for your bank login." A receipt strip of what she never does.
  • 21Receipt policeFor the owner tired of chasing: "Retire from the receipt police." Hand Penny the badge.
